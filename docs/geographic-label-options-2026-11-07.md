# Primary-source options for provisional geographic labels

**Research date:** 2026-11-07  
**Purpose:** Compare source-defined scopes for five labels currently marked provisional in the [geographic convention audit](geographic-conventions-audit.md). This note records options and differences; it does not choose a product definition or change application code.

## Summary

| Requested label | Exact source-defined options found | What the options mean | Current application policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern Europe / Scandinavia | UN M49 Northern Europe (16 country/area records); Nordic cooperation region (8 countries/territories) | M49 is a statistical grouping, not “Scandinavia.” The Nordic grouping is an institutional/cultural cooperation region. The authoritative sources consulted do not establish a single canonical boundary for Scandinavia itself. | M49 subregion 154 (16) |
| Southern Europe / Mediterranean | UN M49 Southern Europe (16); Barcelona Convention’s Mediterranean Sea Area and its 21 state parties (plus EU as a party) | M49 defines an exclusive statistical subregion. The Barcelona Convention defines a marine area and participation in a treaty, not a land region named “Southern Europe.” | M49 subregion 039 (16) |
| Gulf States | GCC member states (6) | Exact political/institutional set. “Gulf States” used as a geographic expression can have a broader littoral meaning; the GCC source does not define all Gulf-bordering states as its members. | Six GCC members |
| Oceania / South Pacific | UN M49 Oceania (29 country/area records); Pacific Islands Forum membership (18 members) | M49 is the full statistical region and includes Australia/New Zealand. The Forum is a political grouping, not an ocean-boundary definition; it also includes Australia and New Zealand. “South Pacific” is not equivalent to either set. | M49 region 009 (29) |
| Pacific Islands | UN M49 Melanesia + Micronesia + Polynesia (23 records); Pacific Islands Forum (18 members) | M49 provides a geographic-statistical island grouping excluding Australia/New Zealand. ## Northern Europe / Scandinavia

**UN statistical option.** UNSD M49 subregion 154, Northern Europe, contains 16 country/area records: Åland Islands, Denmark, Estonia, Faroe Islands, Finland, Guernsey, Iceland, Ireland, Isle of Man, Jersey, Latvia, Lithuania, Norway, Svalbard and Jan Mayen Islands, Sweden, and the United Kingdom. The M49 source says these regions are for statistical purposes and that assignment does not imply political or other affiliation. This set includes the Baltic states, Ireland, the UK and Crown Dependencies; it does not include Greenland. It is consequently broader than ordinary narrow uses of “Scandinavia.” [UNSD M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/)

**Nordic institutional option.** The Nordic Council of Ministers’ Nordic cooperation source defines the Nordic Region as Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, and Åland. That is eight members/entities in the source’s regional description. Compared with M49 Northern Europe, it adds Greenland and omits Estonia, Guernsey, Ireland, the Isle of Man, Jersey, Latvia, Lithuania, Svalbard/Jan Mayen and the UK; it shares Denmark, Faroe Islands, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden and Åland. [Nordic Council of Ministers: Facts about the Nordic countries](https://www.norden.org/en/information/facts-about-nordic-countries)

**Scandinavia limit.** The official Nordic source located defines the *Nordic Region*, not a bounded *Scandinavia* region. M49 defines *Northern Europe*, not Scandinavia. These sources therefore support the two exact options above but do not supply a single official, canonical Scandinavia list. A product label that joins “Northern Europe / Scandinavia” combines a broad statistical grouping with a distinct and variably used regional term.

## Southern Europe / Mediterranean

**UN statistical option.** M49 subregion 039, Southern Europe, contains 16 records: Albania, Andorra, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Gibraltar, Greece, Holy See, Italy, Malta, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Portugal, San Marino, Serbia, Slovenia, and Spain. This is an exclusive statistical grouping; “Mediterranean” is not part of its M49 name or definition. [UNSD M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/)

**Marine-convention option.** The Barcelona Convention defines the “Mediterranean Sea Area” as the maritime waters of the Mediterranean Sea proper, including its gulfs and seas, bounded west by the meridian through Cape Spartel at the entrance to the Strait of Gibraltar and east by the southern limits of the Dardanelles. The Convention may be extended to coastal areas as each party defines within its own territory. This is a marine jurisdiction, not a list of all countries that should be called Southern Europe. [Barcelona Convention, Article 3](https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/7096/BarcelonaConvention_Consolidated_eng.pdf)

UNEP/MAP lists 22 Convention parties: 21 states—Albania, Algeria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Cyprus, Egypt, France, Greece, Israel, Italy, Lebanon, Libya, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Morocco, Slovenia, Spain, Syrian Arab Republic, Tunisia, and Türkiye—plus the European Union. The 21-state party set and M49 Southern Europe overlap on nine states: Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Greece, Italy, Malta, Montenegro, Slovenia, and Spain. Barcelona parties additionally include Algeria, Cyprus, Egypt, France, Israel, Lebanon, Libya, Monaco, Morocco, Syria, Tunisia and Türkiye; M49 Southern Europe additionally includes Andorra, Gibraltar, Holy See, North Macedonia, Portugal, San Marino and Serbia. A party list is not itself a shoreline or airport boundary. [UNEP/MAP Contracting Parties](https://www.unep.org/unepmap/node/19634)

**Meaning limit.** “Southern Europe” and “Mediterranean” describe overlapping but non-identical areas. The M49 option excludes Mediterranean coastal states assigned outside Europe, while the Barcelona source is limited to sea waters and party jurisdiction. The sources do not establish one combined “Southern Europe / Mediterranean” country list.

## Gulf States

**Institutional option.** GCC’s official charter and member-state material name six members: Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates. These are the exact countries in the application’s GCC-based option. [GCC official member states](https://www.gcc-sg.org/en/AboutUs/MemberStates/Pages/default.aspx) and [GCC Charter, official site](https://www.gcc-sg.org/en/AboutUs/Pages/default.aspx)

**Meaning limit.** GCC membership is an unambiguous political membership set; it is not a coastline definition. The generic phrase “Gulf States” can be read geographically as states bordering a named Gulf, which may differ from GCC membership. The GCC source establishes its six members but does not claim that every geographic Gulf littoral state is a member or that the generic label has a single definition. The options note can therefore state “six GCC member states” precisely, while any broader Gulf-coast interpretation would require an explicit named water body and boundary convention.

## Oceania / South Pacific

**UN statistical option.** M49 region 009, Oceania, contains 29 country/area records: Australia, Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Heard Island and McDonald Islands, New Zealand, Norfolk Island; Fiji, New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu; Guam, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, United States Minor Outlying Islands; American Samoa, Cook Islands, French Polynesia, Niue, Pitcairn, Samoa, Tokelau, Tonga, Tuvalu, and Wallis and Futuna Islands. UNSD explains that its geographic groupings are for statistical use and lists one region for each country or area; the FAQ notes that Oceania was reorganized in 1999 after the Pacific Islands Trust Territory ended. [UNSD M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/)

**Pacific Islands Forum option.** The Forum Secretariat’s 2025 progress report identifies 18 members: Australia, Cook Islands, Fiji, French Polynesia, Kiribati, Nauru, Federated States of Micronesia, Niue, New Caledonia, New Zealand, Palau, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Republic of the Marshall Islands, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu, and Vanuatu. It separately identifies Tokelau, Wallis and Futuna, Guam, and American Samoa as associate members in that report. The Forum is an institutional membership; Australia and New Zealand are members, although they are not ordinarily what a travel user means by “South Pacific islands.” [Pacific Islands Forum Secretariat, 2025 Progress Report](https://forumsec.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/2025%20Progress%20Report%20on%20the%20RCAs%20for%20the%202050%20Strategy%20for%20the%20Blue%20Pacific%20Continent_WEB.pdf)

**Meaning limit.** “Oceania” is a defined M49 statistical region; “South Pacific” is a directional ocean-region phrase and does not have the same extent by definition. M49 includes Australia/New Zealand; PIF membership includes them too but selects political members and omits many other M49 areas. Neither source supports treating the slash expression as one canonical country set.

## Pacific Islands

**M49 geographic-statistical option.** Combining subregions 054 Melanesia, 057 Micronesia, and 061 Polynesia yields 23 country/area records: Fiji, New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu; Guam, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, United States Minor Outlying Islands; American Samoa, Cook Islands, French Polynesia, Niue, Pitcairn, Samoa, Tokelau, Tonga, Tuvalu, and Wallis and Futuna Islands. It excludes the six Australia and New Zealand subregion records. This is a reproducible geographic-statistical choice, not an institutional membership list. [UNSD M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/)

**Forum institutional option.** The Pacific Islands Forum has 18 members (listed in the preceding section), including Australia and New Zealand. Compared with the 23 M49 Melanesia/Micronesia/Polynesia entries, it includes Australia and New Zealand but does not include Guam, Northern Mariana Islands, United States Minor Outlying Islands, American Samoa, Pitcairn, Tokelau, or Wallis and Futuna; it includes Cook Islands and Niue, which are M49 Polynesia entries. The Forum’s 2025 report lists Tokelau, Wallis and Futuna, Guam, and American Samoa as associate members, not full members. [Pacific Islands Forum Secretariat, 2025 Progress Report](https://forumsec.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/2025%20Progress%20Report%20on%20the%20RCAs%20for%20the%202050%20Strategy%20for%20the%20Blue%20Pacific%20Continent_WEB.pdf)

**Meaning limit.** “Pacific Islands” may be read as a geographic island set, an institutional grouping, or a travel-market label. M49 and PIF differ materially and neither should be presented as the uniquely correct ordinary-language boundary. “South Pacific” also should not be used as a synonym for M49 Polynesia: M49 divides Oceania into Australia/New Zealand, Melanesia, Micronesia, and Polynesia, while PIF spans island groups across the Pacific and includes Australia/New Zealand.

## Options and open decision points (no selection made)

- For **Northern Europe / Scandinavia**, retain the current broad M49 Northern Europe list; rename it to “Nordic Region” and use the Nordic Council’s eight-country/territory list; or expose Northern Europe and Scandinavia/Nordic as distinct choices. The sources establish exact M49 and Nordic sets but do not settle a canonical Scandinavia boundary.
- For **Southern Europe / Mediterranean**, retain M49 Southern Europe; define a Mediterranean-coast search using a named shoreline or an explicitly adopted coastal-state list; or split the labels. The Barcelona Convention’s marine limits and party roster must not be conflated.
- For **Gulf States**, specify that the label means GCC membership (six); or select a broader geographic littoral rule that explicitly names which Gulf and how transcontinental or island territories are treated.
- For **Oceania / South Pacific**, retain M49 Oceania; or split M49 Oceania from a separately defined South Pacific scope. PIF membership could be offered as an institutional filter, but it is not equivalent to either geographic phrase.
- For **Pacific Islands**, retain M49 subregions 054/057/061; or offer a separate Pacific Islands Forum membership option. If the Forum option is used, preserve its full 18-member membership and identify associate members separately rather than silently merging them.
