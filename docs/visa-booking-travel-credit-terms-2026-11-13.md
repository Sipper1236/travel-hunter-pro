# Visa Infinite / Booking.com Travel Credit public-terms check — 2026-11-13

**Checked:** 2026-11-13 23:45 UTC  
**Scope:** Public Visa Canada and Booking.com pages only. No Visa/Booking.com account, card data, membership data, or booking was accessed or submitted.

## What the official public sources establish

- Visa Canada's [Visa Infinite benefits page](https://www.visa.ca/en_CA/pay-with-visa/cards/credit-cards/infinite.html) advertises up to 8% (maximum USD 230) in Travel Credits for future stays after signing up for a free Booking.com account and purchasing qualifying accommodations with an eligible Visa Infinite card. The page does not characterize this as an immediate reduction to the accommodation's displayed payable price.
- Visa Canada's [Visa Infinite Privilege page](https://www.visa.ca/en_CA/pay-with-visa/cards/credit-cards/infinite-privilege.html) advertises the same headline amount for qualifying accommodations purchased with an eligible Visa Infinite Privilege card.
- Visa's [Visa Infinite general terms](https://www.visa.ca/en_CA/pay-with-visa/cards/credit-cards/infinite/terms-and-conditions.html) require an eligible, valid Canadian-issued Visa Infinite or Visa Infinite+ card for benefits covered by those terms; some benefits require enrollment. Visa also says offers are non-transferable, discounts cannot be combined, and offers can be changed or cancelled without notice. It directs users to the Visa Infinite Concierge for full third-party supplier terms.
- The public Visa-to-Booking.com link was followed once. The browser text view reached Booking.com and displayed a JavaScript/robot-verification page before any promotion-specific terms. No challenge was attempted or bypassed. Visa's public terms page did not contain Booking.com-specific conditions.

## Conditions not established by the accessible public terms

The public pages reviewed do not specify:

- the promotion's start/end date, booking window, or eligible stay/travel dates;
- which properties, room types, payment options, currencies, or rates qualify, or whether prepaid/non-refundable rates qualify;
- whether 8% is calculated before/after taxes, fees, discounts, or other credits;
- the credit issuance timing, delivery method, expiry, redemption flow, or whether the USD 230 cap is per account, card, booking, or another period;
- the detailed interaction with Booking.com Genius/member rates, property discounts, coupons, refunds, cancellations, amendments, no-shows, partial stays, or chargebacks;
- whether the general Visa “discounts cannot be combined” rule applies to future Booking.com Travel Credits in every stacking scenario;
- whether every Visa Infinite, Infinite+, and Infinite Privilege product is eligible under the same promotion terms. Separate Visa benefit pages advertise the promotion for different product labels, while the linked general terms listed above speak to Infinite/Infinite+.

Do not infer these missing terms from the headline offer, a Booking.com login flow, indexed snippets, third-party pages, or unrelated Visa hotel benefits. Visa states that complete third-party terms are available through its Concierge; obtaining or verifying those terms would require an authorized accessible source.

## Product and evidence consequence

Keep the offer as an observed public promotion summary only. Do not subtract an assumed credit from a hotel price, label it an immediate discount, or mark a cardholder, property, stay, rate, or booking as eligible without the full current terms and matching offer evidence. A like-for-like room quote and user eligibility were not checked.
