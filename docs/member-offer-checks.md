## What the checker verifies

The user opts in to individual public sources. The app reads official public pages and requires known text markers before it reports `information_observed`. The UI offers separate opt-ins for CAA Atlantic, CAA-Québec, CAA South Central Ontario, CAA North & East Ontario, CAA Saskatchewan, CAA Manitoba, CAA Niagara, BCAA British Columbia and Yukon, and AMA Alberta. BCAA's official Rewards hotel-category page is selectable, but the application fetcher currently receives only a JavaScript shell and therefore reports `unverified`; search-indexed offer text is not substituted. The Saskatchewan source is a Fall 2025 magazine page and is explicitly dated information, not proof that its offer is current. This is broader club coverage, not nationwide coverage. Requests use the shared persisted provider pacing/cooldown policy; a 429 is exposed as `rate_limited` with a retry time and prevents another request while the cooldown is active. For CAA Atlantic and Visa Infinite the app also fetches the linked terms page and reports that evidence separately as `termsStatus`. If a terms request fails or markers change, the public offer can remain observed while terms are marked `unverified`.

The UI does not ask for or retain membership numbers, card details, or accounts. Source checks do not establish that the traveler qualifies, that a selected property/date/rate participates, or that an offer is stackable. They do not query a user account, price a comparable stay, or apply a discount.
