## Europe popularity exclusion correction — 2026-11-09

A current-code review found that Europe intentionally excluded the first three airport candidates after sorting, even when fewer than three had recorded passenger data. Since unranked candidates followed alphabetically, this could treat unknown popularity as if it were a popularity rank and exclude airports such as AMS/CDG despite only LHR having traffic evidence. The resolver now excludes up to three candidates with positive recorded traffic only, retains airports without passenger figures after the ranked candidates, and reports the requested limit, known-ranked count, and exclusion shortfall. Region results display that shortfall and state unknown-popularity airports were retained. No traffic values are fabricated and no unranked airport is claimed to be popularity-ranked.

Regression evidence: new bounded Europe fixture fails on the old exclusion behavior and passes with the corrected rule; `node --test tests/m49-regions.test.mjs tests/search.test.mjs tests/ourairports.test.mjs` passed 30/30; full `npm test` passed 529/529; production build passed; `npm audit --audit-level=low` found 0 vulnerabilities; rebuilt `check:region-coverage` passed on loopback port 3101 with provider/job traffic intercepted and verified shortfall disclosure. No live airport or fare lookup occurred. If fewer than three airports have recorded passenger counts, fewer than three are excluded; meeting the three-airport preference then requires better traffic data.

For M49 scopes, the current English table at the [UN Statistics Division M49 overview](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/) was fetched directly and parsed using the repository parser; the listed sets are the resulting current country/area rows, shown as name (M49 code, ISO alpha-2 when assigned). A fresh 2026-11-07 fetch parsed 248 rows; an exact M49-code set comparison against every documented table row found zero membership mismatches across all 28 policies. For Middle East, Gulf States and Levant & Beyond, the table shows the application’s explicit ISO/custom scope applied against those same rows. M49 is a statistical country/area hierarchy, not a legal border or airport-boundary dataset.

The Gulf States code is the six GCC ISO areas (BH, KW, OM, QA, SA, AE), corresponding to the [GCC official member-states page](https://www.gcc-sg.org/en/AboutUs/MemberStates/Pages/default.aspx). The GCC webpage is dynamically rendered; the application’s membership is explicit in code. Custom Middle East and Levant membership is defined in the repository, not asserted as universal terminology.

## Airport resolution: current policy and evidence

Country/area membership is resolved to Wikidata country entities and then to bounded airport observations. Except Europe, the airport scope is country-level; airports in transcontinental countries can therefore fall outside a common geographic understanding of a named region. M49 assignment alone does not establish airport location or airport completeness.

Europe now uses M49 region 150 membership, then filters airport candidates with the approximate coordinate rectangle 25°W–45°E and 34°N–72°N. M49 places Russia in Europe but Türkiye and Cyprus in Western Asia, so this policy excludes Turkish and Cypriot airports even where common definitions include them. The box is a product convention, not an authoritative continental boundary: it can include/exclude edge cases and does not split airport grounds at borders. ## Interpretation and limits

- The M49 sets can overlap only where the requested scopes combine different hierarchy levels or custom additions; overlap notes are stated in the table.
- “Northern Europe / Scandinavia,” “Southern Europe / Mediterranean,” “Oceania / South Pacific,” and “Pacific Islands” continue to use the exact M49 policies in the table; they are not assertions that those compound terms have universal boundaries. Common aliases now have explicit separately documented scopes and user-facing caveats.
- “Middle East” and “Levant & Beyond” are application conventions. Türkiye/Cyprus/Caucasus are included by the former through M49 Western Asia; the latter explicitly includes Cyprus, Egypt, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, State of Palestine, Syria and Türkiye, while excluding the Gulf six and Iran.
- The table is a country/area policy audit. It does not assert every airport has been found, nor that fare providers cover every included route or hold simultaneous inventory. The 28 requested labels are present as supported resolver labels; this is not evidence of exhaustive airport or fare coverage.
