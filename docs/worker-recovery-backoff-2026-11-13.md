# Durable worker lease recovery backoff — 2026-11-13

Expired flight, comparison, and calendar worker leases are now requeued with a durable `next_attempt_at`. The first abandoned lease waits 1 second; the next waits 2 seconds. The delay grows exponentially and is capped at 30 seconds. Recovery count remains bounded at two; a further abandoned attempt becomes a terminal timeout instead of restarting indefinitely. The new timestamp is persisted in SQLite and survives a store restart. Existing checkpoints remain available to the resumed worker.

Package and hotel jobs keep their separate browser-session rule: an expired browser lease ends the job rather than replaying a session that cannot be reconstructed. Intentional pause/resume does not consume the crash-recovery count. Provider-level request pacing and retry-after handling remain separate from this worker-process backoff.
